Binker privacy policy · Effective November 6, 2026

Binker is a portfolio tracker that measures investments in Bitcoin. This policy explains what we process when you use Binker, why we need it, and how to manage or delete it.

The essentials: your account and manually entered portfolio records are stored with our backend providers. We do not sell your personal information or send your portfolio records to market-data providers. You can delete your account from the app.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: your verified email address, account identifier, and identity information supplied by the sign-in method you choose. Some sign-in providers may supply a private relay email address instead of your regular address. We do not receive your sign-in provider's password.
  • Portfolio records: the assets, quantities, prices, dates, currencies, fees, deposits, withdrawals, conversions, income, splits, and corrections you enter, together with records needed to process saves and retries.
  • Market-data requests: asset search text, listing identifiers, and date ranges used to retrieve prices and calculate your portfolio views.
  • Technical and support information: information you send to support and operational information such as request timestamps, errors, and security events. Hosting and authentication providers may also process IP addresses and device or network information to provide and protect their services.

Binker does not connect to brokerage accounts or wallets, execute trades, request wallet private keys, or import banking credentials.

2. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, save and retrieve your records, calculate balances and performance, retrieve market prices, prevent unauthorized access, resolve failed requests, answer support questions, and comply with applicable obligations. Entering records does not authorize Binker to move money or place trades.

3. Service providers and sharing

  • Backend providers provide hosting, account authentication, and database storage for Binker.
  • Sign-in providers verify your identity when you choose their sign-in method. Email delivery providers deliver authentication emails when you use email sign-in.
  • Market-data providers receive requests from our backend for asset searches, market prices, and historical rates. We do not include your account identity, transaction quantities, portfolio balances, or private portfolio records in those requests.
  • Infrastructure and support providers may process information needed to operate the service, deliver authentication or support communications, and prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal information. The Binker app does not currently include advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or third-party behavioral analytics. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

4. Your choices and account deletion

You can review, correct, and delete portfolio entries in the app. To permanently delete your account, open Portfolio → Account → Delete account and follow the confirmation steps. If a linked sign-in provider requires authorization revocation, you may be asked to confirm that identity so we can revoke Binker’s authorization before completing deletion.

Successful account deletion removes your active account and private portfolio records. If deletion cannot be completed, the app displays an error and lets you retry; it does not claim that your data has been deleted. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your backend account.

Different verified emails can identify different accounts. In particular, a private relay address supplied by a sign-in provider may differ from an email you previously used with Binker.

5. Retention and backups

We keep your account and portfolio records while your account is active, unless you delete individual records or your account. Operational and support records are kept only as needed to provide support, protect the service, resolve problems, and meet applicable obligations.

If we make manual application-data backups, access is restricted and backup copies are removed within 30 days. Deleted records may remain in those backups until they expire. Restoring a backup must not reactivate a deleted account or restore its private records into an active account. Public instruments and market-price data are shared service data and may be retained independently of your account.

6. Security and international processing

Binker uses encrypted network connections, secure device storage for native sign-in sessions, and account-based backend access controls. Backend credentials and provider authorization keys are kept on the server. Our service providers may process information in countries other than where you live, under the protections and obligations applicable to those services.

7. Privacy requests and children

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before acting on it.

Binker is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Binker, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Binker or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. We will provide additional notice when required for a material change.
